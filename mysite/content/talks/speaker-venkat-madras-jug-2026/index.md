# Event Summary

![Madras JUG with Venkat Subramaniam](images/venkat_madras_event_2026/venkat_madras_event_2026_2.jpg)

I had the privilege of presenting our open-source initiative and the development of SQLComponent, a Java compile-time ORM, in front of Venkat Subramaniam at the MadrasJUG event. It was a great pleasure meeting him again and being part of such an inspiring session.

His insights on virtual threads and their real-time use cases had a significant impact on my learning. Listening to his experiences and perspectives added immense value and clarity to my understanding.

We traveled from Coimbatore to attend this event and share our work with the community, making the entire experience even more memorable and fulfilling.

![Madras JUG event](images/venkat_madras_event_2026/venkat_madras_event_2026_1.jpg)

# My Contribution

![Presenting SQLComponent](images/venkat_madras_event_2026/venkat_madras_event_2026_3.jpg)

I had the opportunity to present and speak during the session, which was both exciting and nerve-wracking. Standing on the same stage where Venkat Subramaniam spoke was a truly unforgettable moment for me.

I presented our work on SQLComponent and shared insights into building a Java compile-time ORM. It was an honor to contribute to the community and be recognized for the efforts.

I would like to thank Thamizh and Venkat for providing me with this opportunity. I am also grateful to Sathish and Raja, my seniors from the Tamil Nadu Java User Group, for their continuous support and belief in me.

![Community at Madras JUG](images/venkat_madras_event_2026/venkat_madras_event_2026_4.jpg)

This experience will remain one of the most memorable milestones in my journey, and I look forward to many more such opportunities in the future.
