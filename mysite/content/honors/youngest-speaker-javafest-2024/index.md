## Youngest Speaker
**Issued by:** Bangalore Java User Group · Nov 2024  
**Associated with:** JAVAFEST

It was an absolute honor to represent Quinbay - Blibli.com at JavaFest 2024 and share the stage with some of the brightest minds in tech. Standing before industry leaders to discuss the "Missing pieces of Java persistence puzzles" and its evolving framework was both humbling and exhilarating. This experience reinforced the value of collaborative learning, where we grow together as a community.
