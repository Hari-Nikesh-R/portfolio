## What you can contribute

- improvements to existing pages (clarity, examples, structure)
- new workshop runbooks
- talk outlines + demo notes
- “what I learned” blog posts

## Contribution standards (lightweight)

- **Be specific**: include steps, examples, or checklists
- **Be kind**: assume good intent, write for newcomers too
- **Be reusable**: prefer templates and patterns

## Page template (copy/paste)

- **Outcome**: what the reader gets
- **Prereqs**: what they should know
- **Steps**: ordered, short
- **Links**: internal links to related pages

## Where to start

- First contribution? Improve a single section in the [JUG Playbook](/community/java-user-group-playbook/).
