# Event Summary

The Value-Added Course was inaugurated at VET Institute of Arts and Science (Co-ed) College, Erode, with the objective of encouraging students to develop industry-ready skills beyond the academic curriculum.

![Inaugural Session](images/gs_vet_2026/gs_vet_2026_2.png)

The inaugural session brought together the Principal, faculty members, and students from the first, second, and third years. The event focused on inspiring students to embrace continuous learning, innovation, and professional growth.

The keynote session emphasized the importance of curiosity as the foundation of learning and innovation. Students were encouraged to cultivate a mindset of asking questions, exploring new technologies, building practical projects, and preparing themselves for future careers in a rapidly evolving technology landscape.

The session also highlighted the growing impact of Artificial Intelligence while reinforcing that qualities such as curiosity, consistency, communication, and lifelong learning remain timeless skills for every successful professional.

![Keynote Address](images/gs_vet_2026/gs_vet_2026_3.png)

The event concluded with an interactive discussion where students actively engaged with questions regarding career development, learning strategies, and opportunities in the software industry.
