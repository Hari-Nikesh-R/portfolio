# Event Summary

The Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG) organized an exceptional developer meetup at Thoughtworks, Coimbatore, hosting globally renowned speaker, author, and educator **Dr. Venkat Subramaniam** for an impactful session on **“Finding and Fixing Issues with Legacy Code using AI.”**

![Finding and Fixing Issues with Legacy Code using AI](images/august_ai_meetup_2026/august_ai_meetup_2026_1.jpeg)

The evening was filled with high energy, deep technical insights, and engaging storytelling. Starting with great enthusiasm, the session seamlessly ran until 10:00 PM with the entire room thoroughly captivated from beginning to end.

Venkat demonstrated practical techniques for approaching legacy codebases with modern AI tools, emphasizing the mindset of *"AI is a tool — ask what tool do I need, rather than what can I do with this tool."* Through live problem-solving, relatable examples, and his trademark wit, he broke down complex modernization concepts into clear, actionable principles.

![Interactive Discussion and Key Takeaways](images/august_ai_meetup_2026/august_ai_meetup_2026_3.jpeg)

The meetup served as a powerful gathering for developers, architects, and community members in and around Coimbatore, offering an open space to discuss real-world engineering challenges, AI adoption strategies, and software craftsmanship.

A special thanks to **Thoughtworks** for hosting the event and championing tech community initiatives, and to **Santhosh** from Thoughtworks, Coimbatore, for enabling this meetup on short notice.

# My Contribution / Learnings

Having attended a KochiJUG meetup a year ago where I first experienced Venkat's captivating storytelling and deep technical delivery, this event was an emotional full-circle moment for me as an organizer helping host him in Coimbatore.

![Moments with Venkat Subramaniam](images/august_ai_meetup_2026/august_ai_meetup_2026_4.png)

As an organizer representing the **Tamil Nadu Java User Group**, my key contributions and takeaways included:

- **Coordinating and Hosting:** Facilitated end-to-end event planning, speaker coordination, and hosting for Dr. Venkat Subramaniam in Coimbatore.
- **Venue & Community Collaboration:** Partnered closely with the Thoughtworks Coimbatore team and Santhosh to set up the venue, AV systems, and attendee experience seamlessly at short notice.
- **Engaging the Developer Community:** Managed community outreach, attendee flow, and active moderation during the interactive Q&A segments until late evening.
- **Key Learnings:** Reinforced the power of storytelling in technical education and the profound value of community building in connecting passionate engineers.
