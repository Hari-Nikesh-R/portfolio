# Event Summary

The **Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG) Madurai Chapter Launch** was a full-day technical community meetup bringing together developers, students, engineers, and technology enthusiasts from Madurai and surrounding areas. The event focused on Java, JVM performance, memory management, AI agents, hands-on learning, and community networking.

Hosted at **Dot Com Infoway, Madurai** in collaboration with **TNIA**, the meetup served as a platform for deep technical exploration and marked the official launch of the TNJUG Madurai Chapter — establishing a local hub for continuous knowledge sharing, peer mentorship, and developer community building.

![Welcome Address and Event Kickoff with Dot Com Infoway & Organizers](images/ai_day_madurai_2026/ai_day_madurai_2026_6.png)

The day featured insightful technical sessions and hands-on learning led by industry experts:
- **Vaibhav Choudhary**
- **Pawan Chawdhary**
- **Abdul Salam Mohamed Kani**

The speakers shared their deep expertise covering JVM performance tuning, memory debugging using tools like JMC and JFR, shared libraries and native method stacks, and building modern AI agent architectures.

![Introducing the Java Performance Debugging Session](images/ai_day_madurai_2026/ai_day_madurai_2026_2.png)

Attendees actively participated in practical exercises, asked thought-provoking questions, and connected with fellow engineers throughout the day, creating an energetic and collaborative community environment.

# My Contribution / Learnings

As an organiser of the event and part of the **TNJUG** community, I was involved in taking the Madurai Chapter from an idea to its first successful meetup.

![Core Organizing Team and Volunteers](images/ai_day_madurai_2026/ai_day_madurai_2026_0.png)

- **Chapter Initiation:** Helped conceptualise and establish the TNJUG Madurai Chapter, laying the foundation for an active developer community in southern Tamil Nadu.
- **Event Planning & Coordination:** Planned and coordinated the overall event structure, including speaker lineups, venue logistics, agenda scheduling, hands-on session setups, and attendee engagement.
- **Speaker Coordination:** Coordinated with **Vaibhav Choudhary**, **Pawan Chawdhary**, and **Abdul Salam Mohamed Kani** to bring diverse expertise across JVM performance, memory debugging, and AI engineering.
- **Partnership & Sponsorship Coordination:** Partnered with **Dot Com Infoway** for venue hosting and hospitality, and collaborated with **Rapl** for food sponsorship.
- **Community Building:** Engaged with developers, students, and engineers across Madurai to cultivate an open, supportive culture centered around continuous technical learning and collaboration.
- **Volunteer Coordination & On-Ground Execution:** Worked closely with TNJUG co-organisers and student volunteers to manage logistics, stage handling, and session pacing for a seamless full-day experience.
