# My Contribution / Learnings

I had the opportunity to present a talk titled **"Beyond LLMs: The Evolution from Microservices to Agentic Systems"**, discussing how software architecture is evolving in the age of AI. During the session, I shared insights on how the industry spent years breaking monoliths into microservices, and how we are now teaching AI agents to collaborate with those services. I emphasized that the future is about AI and Software Engineering working together, rather than competing.

![Speaker Session: Beyond LLMs](images/dev_on_speaker_juneblr_jug_2026/dev_on_speaker_juneblr_jug_2026_4.jpg)

To help attendees try out the concepts, I shared a demo along with its source code and guide, encouraging collaborative learning to make this architectural concept stronger. It was inspiring to connect with the community, answer questions, and participate in these forward-looking discussions.

![Demo and Discussion](images/dev_on_speaker_juneblr_jug_2026/dev_on_speaker_juneblr_jug_2026_5.jpg)

The Java community continues to prove that great technology is built through collaboration, learning, and knowledge sharing. A huge thank you to everyone who attended, contributed, and made the meetup memorable. I'm looking forward to the next Bangalore JUG gathering!
