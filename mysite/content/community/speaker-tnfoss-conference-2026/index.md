# Event Summary

**Event:** TNFOSS Conference 2026  
**Role:** Speaker & Open Source Community Representative  
**Representing:** Tamilnadu Java User Group (TNJUG)  

TNFOSS Conference 2026 brought together developers, educators, students, industry leaders, and open-source enthusiasts to promote collaboration, knowledge sharing, and innovation in the open-source ecosystem. The conference featured technical talks, community initiatives, project showcases, and the Open Source Lab, providing a platform to demonstrate real-world open-source impact.

![TNFOSS Conference 2026 Event](images/speaker_tnfoss_2026/speaker_tnfoss_2026_2.JPG)

It was a privilege to participate and present the active community efforts and open-source contributions driven by the Tamilnadu JUG.

---

# My Contribution / Learnings

* **Represented Tamilnadu Java User Group (TNJUG)**: Presented the community's open-source initiatives and shared how developers and students can get involved.
* **Technical Session on SQLComponents**: Delivered a technical session on **SQLComponents**, a Java-based compile-time ORM. I explained its architecture, features, compile-time safety benefits, and practical use cases.
* **Live Demonstration**: Conducted a live demonstration showcasing how SQLComponents simplifies database development, prevents runtime errors, and boosts developer productivity.
* **Empowering the Community**: Highlighted how open-source projects can empower students, developers, educators, and the broader technology community.

![Delivering SQLComponents Session](images/speaker_tnfoss_2026/speaker_tnfoss_2026_6.jpg)

* **Open Source Adoption & Mentoring**: Engaged with attendees by discussing open-source adoption, community building, and collaborative software development.
* **Open Source Lab Contribution**: Contributed at the **Open Source Lab** stall by interacting with students, educators, and contributors, observing innovative projects, and encouraging active participation in open source.
* **Community Networking**: Networked with community leaders and industry professionals, including **Samir Kamerkar (Canonical)**. It was an honor to exchange ideas on mentoring, community growth, and strengthening the open-source ecosystem, receiving valuable guidance and insights.
* **Bridging Academia & Industry**: Promoted collaboration between academia and industry through community-driven initiatives and knowledge sharing.

![Networking and Discussions](images/speaker_tnfoss_2026/speaker_tnfoss_2026_3.jpg)
