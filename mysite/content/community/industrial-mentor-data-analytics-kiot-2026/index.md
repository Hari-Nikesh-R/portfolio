# Event Summary

An academic knowledge-sharing session was conducted at **Knowledge Institute of Technology (KIOT)**, focused on **Data Analytics** and its practical applications. The session provided students with an opportunity to explore data-driven concepts, understand industry perspectives, and interact through discussions around technology and real-world problem solving.

The event also included active interactions with students and an opportunity to learn about their creative and innovative projects, making it an engaging two-way learning and knowledge-sharing experience.

![Interactive Discussion and Industry Perspectives with Students](images/kiot_industrial_mentor_data_analytics_2026/kiot_industrial_mentor_data_analytics_2026_2.jpeg)

# My Contribution / Learnings

I served as the **Data Analytics session speaker and mentor**, sharing insights from my M.Tech learning journey and practical experience with the students. I explained key concepts, discussed real-world applications, addressed student questions, and encouraged them to approach technology through practical problem solving.

- **Data Analytics Concepts & Applications:** Delivered an interactive session exploring key data-driven concepts, practical tools, and how data analytics is applied to solve industry problems.
- **Hands-on Student Mentoring:** Interacted directly with student teams to understand their projects, provided constructive feedback on their ideas, and encouraged their innovative approach to technology.
- **Two-Way Learning:** The experience strongly reflected my belief that **“The best way to learn is to teach”** — while sharing knowledge with the students, I also gained new perspectives and fresh inspiration from their ideas and projects.
- **Practical Problem-Solving Mindset:** Encouraged students to look beyond theoretical definitions and cultivate hands-on experimentation and critical problem-solving skills.
