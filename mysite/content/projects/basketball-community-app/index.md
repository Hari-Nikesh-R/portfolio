## Problem

Local basketball communities often rely on scattered chats and word-of-mouth. The result: missed games, unclear schedules, and limited inclusion for newcomers.

## What the app enables

- **Runs**: schedule games with location, time, and capacity
- **Players**: profiles + skill tags + availability
- **Groups**: local communities with moderators and rules
- **Trust**: lightweight reputation (attendance + sportsmanship)

## Community-first principles

- clear onboarding for new players
- transparent group rules
- events that connect online → offline
