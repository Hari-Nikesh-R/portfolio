## Overview

Blibli is the mobile shopping app by Blibli (PT Global Digital Niaga) for Android. Users can browse, order, and pay for a wide array of goods—groceries, household items, electronics, fashion, beauty, and home decor—along with additional services like gadget/electronics trade-ins.

**Period:** Aug 2025 – Present
