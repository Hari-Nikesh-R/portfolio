## Why this format works

Most candidates fail due to **signal**, not intelligence:

- unclear approach
- weak edge-case thinking
- poor communication under time

## Runbook (60–90 min)

- 10 min: pattern primer + constraints checklist
- 25 min: live mock (candidate drives)
- 10 min: “rewrite the solution” cleanup
- 15 min: redo with better narration

## Scorecard (simple)

- Approach clarity
- Correctness + edge cases
- Complexity awareness
- Communication quality
